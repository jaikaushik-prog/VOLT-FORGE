# Recommendation

**Use 2 LG M50T cells in parallel, with the negative output at the far cell, for the Raspberry Pi 5 gateway backup.** It is the smallest qualifying design among the parallel arrangements tested.

This is a simulation result. Nothing here has been built or measured. Generated by `scripts/write_recommendation.py` from `reports/design_study_v2/run1/summary.json`, software `dd8746b0898bcae0`.

## The result

The mission is 25 W for 10 minutes through an assumed 90% converter, held at 8.42 A, with a 3.3 V floor at 23 °C. A design qualifies only if every required Pi case passes: nominal, mismatch at every cell position in both directions, thermal sensitivities and converter efficiency. ## Why 2 cells

- **One cell is refused.** At 8.42 A it exceeds the 7.275 A limit, in all 15 required cases.
- **2 cells pass every required case.** Worst-case current is 5.33 A, which leaves 1.95 A of margin, and the lowest voltage is 3.50 V.
- **It halves the cell purchase.** ₹4,824 to ₹2,412, and 280 g to 140 g. This is cells only, not a complete backup unit.
- **Temperature does not change the decision.** The worst 2-cell case reaches 31.6 °C, including halved cooling and heat capacity.

## What argues against it

- **It has less margin.** Four cells stay at 3.34 A in their worst required case, against 5.33 A here.
- **The 12 A stress test can fail.** 2 of 62 combined mismatch samples were refused for exceeding the cell limit. All 62 samples passed at the Pi load. Samples show sensitivity, not a probability.
- **No link option wins everywhere.** The opposite-end output gave the best worst-case margin, 5.33 A against 5.49 A. It still raised peak current in 35 of 81 paired cases. The cell count is the real decision, and the output position is a small refinement.
- **Temperatures are not validated.** All 12 pre-declared measured comparison cases missed their bands. Treat temperatures as a range.
- **The board is unconfirmed.** The Geekworm X1206 V2.0 has four 21700 slots. Its listing does not confirm running with fewer than four cells fitted, and it publishes no battery cutoff or efficiency.
