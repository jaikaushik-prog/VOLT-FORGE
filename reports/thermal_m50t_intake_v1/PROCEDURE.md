# M50T thermal archive: documentation and prospective allocation

26 October 2026. This follows the completed synthetic thermal prototype and the
user-authorized download and intake. It does not revise that sealed study.
Before this declaration, only the archive directory, external/internal README
and the published paper were inspected. No measurement member was opened.
Published aggregate CCC results are already known literature, not hidden tests.

1. Pin the original ZIP and external README by SHA-256 and published MD5.
   Inventory ZIP central-directory metadata without extracting or reading any
   measurement payload. Read only the exactly named internal README.
2. Group raw thermal, processed thermal and electrical representations by
   specimen and test ID. These are representations of one experiment, not
   independent samples. Retain literal filenames and flag missing/conflicting
   nominal conditions. Never fill missing metadata silently.
3. Check the primary paper's sensor diagram, protocol and fixture definitions.
   Record channel mapping, units supported by documentation, and remaining
   calibration, heat-input, synchronization and preparation gaps. A chamber
   setpoint is not a recorded ambient temperature. A cold-junction channel is
   not automatically ambient. Fin heat rejection is not transient cell heat
   generation, and CCC is not module-to-ambient conductance.
4. Allocate whole experiment families before any outcome inspection. Cell 1,
   test 103 (nominal 50% SOC, 20 degC), is the first development candidate.
   Other cell-1 families remain unused development reserves. Every cell-2
   family remains reserved for future assessment, including both 10 degC
   records 107 and 108; their relationship must be established before use.
   Filenames alone do not prove repetition, completeness or independent samples.
5. This intake exposes no measurement-reading or fitting command. All numerical
   members remain unopened. The allocation is a reservation, not authorization
   to open assessment: a future analysis must freeze its model, calibration,
   alignment, sequence-selection rules and error criterion first. If the
   necessary physical definitions are unresolved, stop before fitting and
   state the exact evidence required. No arbitrary heat capacity/conductance,
   electrical I-squared-R heat substitution or reference-target tuning.
6. Implement and test metadata-only inventory, strict document allowlisting,
   complete-family allocation and preservation checks. Produce machine-readable
   inventory/allocation and a decision report. Keep raw files/PDFs local.
   Do not modify frozen electrical/thermal source, dependencies or receipts.

Any later development outcome read must be logged as used evidence. A future
cell-2 check would assess another characterized specimen in this laboratory
fixture; it would not establish population-level prediction or four-cell
module accuracy. Application temperature requirements remain unset.
